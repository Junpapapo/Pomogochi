03

Seven Principles

The rules that make the method
effective and repeatable.

Timeboxed

Time is set.
When time is up,
move on.

No Judgment

In the dump phase,
quantity over
quality.

Structure

Distill with simple
frames, not
perfection.

Two Actions

Always end with
up to 2 actionable
next steps.

Repeat

Short sessions,
often repeated
create change.

Low Friction

Use the tool
that's easiest
for you.

Reflect

Check results and
improve the next
session.
