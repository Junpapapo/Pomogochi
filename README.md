## 💡 What is Pomogochi?

**Pomogochi** is a fusion of **POMO** (Pomodoro Focus Timer & Two-Beat Thinking Engine) and **GOCHI** (Your faithful Desktop Pet companion). 

Unlike conventional rigid productivity tools, **Pomogochi** brings warmth, delight, and mental clarity to your daily browser experience. Whether you are studying, coding, writing, or designing, your cozy desktop buddy stays by your side—cheering you on, tracking your focus sessions, and growing together with you.

---

## 🧠 Two-Beat Thinking™ Framework

> **Turn mental clutter into clear action in 10 minutes.**

Pomogochi features **Two-Beat Thinking™**, a proprietary 6-step practical thinking pipeline designed to eliminate cognitive overload:

1. **First Beat (Focus & Brain Dump)**: Discharge all your unorganized thoughts, worries, and ideas freely into the canvas alongside a cozy Pomodoro countdown timer.
2. **Second Beat (Refine & Action)**: Categorize your ideas into 6 structural frame matrices (Eisenhower, Now/Next/Later, MoSCoW, Control, Cause/Effect, Emotion/Fact) and extract **immediate, concrete action items**.

* 🎯 **Step 0: Problem Framing** — Define the core problem for your thinking session.
* 💡 **Step 1: Brain Dump** — Unlimited idea chip entry with your buddy cheering you on.
* 🧩 **Step 2: Frame Matrix Classification** — Drag & drop items into 6 structured frameworks.
* ✨ **Step 3: Insight Refinement** — Summarize key conclusions and core takeaways.
* 🚩 **Step 4: Action Items Export** — Automatically sync actionable tasks to your main Todo list.
* 📊 **Step 5: Review & Save** — Check session stats, mood emoji ratings, and 3D icon summaries.

Meet **20+ unique, adorable desktop buddies** living right on your browser web pages!
